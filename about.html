Our Story

Quiet luxury, worn with intent.

Founded in 2016 between two cities, Retro Bella designs small-run pieces for a wardrobe that moves easily from morning meetings to late dinners — without ever raising its voice.

Vogue WWD Business of Fashion Harper's Bazaar The Cut
What We Stand For

Three Pillars

01

Craftsmanship

Every garment is cut in small runs and finished by hand in ateliers we've worked with for over a decade. Nothing leaves the studio until it earns the label.

02

Considered Materials

Double-faced wool, grain leather, and washed silk — chosen for how they age, not just how they photograph. Built to be worn for years, not seasons.

03

Slow by Design

We release two collections a year, not twelve. Smaller runs mean less waste, tighter quality control, and pieces that don't feel disposable.

Luxury isn't a logo you can read from across the room. It's a seam that still lies flat after ten years.

— The Retro Bella Design Principle

Our Timeline

2016

The First Room

Retro Bella opens in a single studio in New York's Garment District with a three-piece capsule collection.

2019

Paris Atelier

A second atelier opens in Paris, bringing hand-finishing traditions from a century-old leather workshop into the fold.

2022

The Icon Bag

Our first leather goods line launches, anchored by a hand-finished tote that becomes the house's signature carry.
